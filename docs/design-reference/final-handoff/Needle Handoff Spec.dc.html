Needle — redesign handoff

The mockups show the end state; this is the part they can't show — the tokens behind them, the interactions that only exist as motion, and the decisions someone will otherwise have to re-litigate. Read it alongside Needle Redesign.dc.html; option ids like 3a and 5d refer to that file.

Canonical Home — 3a · Nothing in here has been pushed to the repo

1 · The idea in one paragraph

Home is an issue, not a feed. It carries a number and a date, and turning it (the ‹ › arrows in the masthead) swaps both the lead composition and the editorial rule that selected it. Everything below the lead is the archive seen from different angles: the shelf, one rotating module, the year history. The visual language is a print magazine — Bodoni display, a numbered margin column, catalog references set in mono — applied to a personal listening record.

The accent is deliberately near-silent: one underline, one focus ring, one chart series. If a third accent use appears, something else should give one up. No gradients, no tinted cards, no colored buttons.

3 · Type

Three faces, one job each. Bodoni Moda (400) is display only — headlines, album titles, the selected year, and italic for editorial asides like the issue rule. Archivo is every piece of UI and body text. IBM Plex Mono is metadata: eyebrows, catalog refs, nav, margin numerals — always uppercase with .14–.18em tracking. A face never crosses into another's job.

4 · Grid & spacing

Desktop page gutter is 56px; the left 200px margin column is structural, not decorative — it holds the roman section numeral (I, II, III, IV) and a two-line note, and it is what makes the page read as a spread. Sections are separated by a hairline plus 16–34px of lead-in, never by cards or shadows. Artwork is always aspect-ratio: 1; the lead hero carries 0 20px 46px rgba(23,22,26,.18), grid items a lighter 0 14px 32px rgba(23,22,26,.13), shelf thumbs none. Hover on any artwork is translateY(−4px) over .18s ease — the only hover motion in the system. Mobile collapses to a 20px gutter and turns margin notes into inline rules above their section.

5 · Interactions the mockups can't show

Turning the issue

The ‹ › arrows advance through a fixed rotation of three lead templates paired with three story rules (A: portrait + rail; B: stacked with a pair; C: full-width headline + trio). Template and rule advance together — they are one unit, not two independent axes. The issue number and date change with them, and the rule is always restated in the margin so the selection is never unexplained. Rotation is authored/scheduled server-side, not random per load: two people opening Needle on the same day should see the same issue.

Scroll snapping — decided

Home has exactly one vertical snap point, at the top of the issue bar, so the cover composition parks intact and the issue-turn arrows stay put while you use them. Everything below it scrolls free.

  • scroll-snap-type: y proximity — never mandatory; the reader must always be able to stop mid-section.
  • scroll-padding-top must equal the real sticky masthead height (84px desktop). Mismatch parks the issue bar behind the masthead — this bit us once.
  • Horizontal shelves keep x proximity with scroll-snap-align: start per item.
  • Library, History, Explore and Album detail get no vertical snapping — they are scan-and-hunt surfaces, and snapping fights the eye.

Year chart (History, 4a)

Bars are click-to-select, not hover-preview; the selected year fills in ink and updates the panel to its right (album count, Full Plays). Bar height is scaled against the single busiest year (142 in the sample data), so the chart re-scales as data grows rather than clipping. Keyboard: left/right arrows move selection, and the selected bar must carry the accent focus ring.

6 · States (5d)

The empty archive is the one that matters: a new user has no issue, so Home shows the masthead, an italic Bodoni line explaining what will appear, and a single import action — not a zero-state illustration. Loading uses slot blocks at the artwork's true aspect ratio with no spinner and no shimmer; the layout must not move when art arrives. Errors are set as a margin note in mono beside the affected section, and never replace the whole page. Focus is a 2px accent outline at 3px offset, everywhere, including artwork tiles. Mobile hit targets are 44px minimum — the issue arrows are 44×32 with a 44px tap area.

7 · Open questions

  1. Artwork rights and resolution. Every slot in the mockups is a placeholder. Leads need ~1200px square; shelf thumbs 296px. Source and licensing are unresolved.
  2. Dark mode. Not designed. The palette inverts badly as-is (Bodoni hairlines vanish); it needs its own pass, not a filter.
  3. Rotation cadence. Daily? Weekly? Per session? This determines whether the issue number is a real sequence or cosmetic.
  4. Rule authoring. Three story rules exist. Who writes the fourth, and are they code or content?
  5. Thin archives. Templates B and C need 3 and 4 albums respectively. What does the lead do for a user with two records?
